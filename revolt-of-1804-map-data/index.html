Revolt of 1804 map data

The hex maps of Revolt of 1804 are built from OpenStreetMap and from SRTM elevation data. As the Open Database License asks of a game that uses a database made from OpenStreetMap, here are the game's map tables and the scripts that made them.

  • SerbiaData.swiftKarađorđe's Serbia, 1806–1813, and the Ottoman lands round it: 104 × 83 hexes of 4 km. Made by make_serbia.py.
  • DistrictData.swiftThe Belgrade pashaluk in 1804, from Šabac to Požarevac and from Belgrade to Jagodina: 68 × 58 hexes of 2.5 km. Made by make_district.py.
  • OrasacData.swiftThe Orašac district round Topola in 1804: 68 × 58 hexes of 0.87 km. Made by make_orasac.py.
  • make_serbia.py · make_district.py · make_orasac.pyThe scripts, in Python 3 with nothing beyond its standard library.

The heights come from SRTM 90 m elevation data by NASA and the USGS, which is in the public domain, fetched through OpenTopoData.

How the tables were made

Each script downloads the rivers and places in its map's area from OpenStreetMap through the Overpass API, and the heights through the OpenTopoData API. It then lays a grid of hexes over the area and decides each hex's terrain. Forest, fields, orchards and meadows are not in any survey of the time, so the scripts model them from the heights, the rivers and the places. The places themselves are chosen by hand, in each script's list of places.

The scripts were run from the root of the game's own repository, for example python3 Tools/district/make_serbia.py, and write the tables into the game's sources. To run them elsewhere, change the output path near the top of each script. Downloads are cached beside the script, so later runs only redo the classification.
