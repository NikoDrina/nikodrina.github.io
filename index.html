How do I get help with an app?

Email zorya.support@gmail.com. It comes straight to me, and I answer every message myself.

Do the apps track me or show ads?

Revolt of 1804, Clock Out and Zorya collect no data and show no ads. Break Glass is free, with optional rewarded ads to revive.

Where is my data stored?

Revolt of 1804 keeps your campaigns on your iPhone or iPad and doesn't connect to the internet at all. Clock Out keeps your factory on your iPhone and sends nothing anywhere. Zorya syncs privately through your own iCloud, with no account and no password, and has full JSON export and import built in.

What does Clock Out cost?

The first 31 in-game days of every company are free: the game's calendar moves on as you finish each day, not in real time. After that, one purchase (€1.99) unlocks the full game for good, with no ads and no subscription. To restore it on a new phone, open Settings → Full game → Restore purchase.

What devices are supported?

Revolt of 1804 is made for iPhone and iPad, in landscape, with iOS 17 or later. Clock Out is made for iPhone, with iOS 17 or later. Break Glass and Zorya run on iPhone and iPad, and on Apple-silicon Macs. Each App Store listing shows the minimum system version.
